Privacy Policy — Journal with Will

Effective 16 August 2026

Journal with Will is a journaling app made by one person. This policy is short because the honest answer is short: the app collects nothing, and what you write goes nowhere except your own iCloud.

What the app collects

Nothing. Journal with Will has no accounts, no login, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. We never see your name, your email address, your writing, or anything else. There is nothing to see, because there is nowhere for it to arrive.

There is no server of ours for the app to talk to, and no code in it that could reach one. The only thing that ever leaves your phone is the copy of your own journal that goes to your own iCloud, described below — and if you turn iCloud off, the app works with no network at all.

What stays on your device

Everything the app remembers is stored on your iPhone: your journal entries, the name and look of your companion, which souvenirs and items you have collected, your ink balance, the days you have written, and your settings. Deleting the app deletes all of it from that device.

Your journal, and iCloud

If your iPhone is signed into iCloud, the app keeps a copy of your journal in your own private iCloud database, so that a new phone or a reinstall simply remembers where you were. This is the one place anything you write ever leaves the phone, and it is worth being exact about what that means:

  • The copy lives in your personal iCloud account, run by Apple under Apple's own privacy terms.
  • It is never sent to us. We could not receive it if we wanted to — the app has no server of any kind, and no code in it that talks to one.
  • We cannot read it. Apple's private database is private to the account that owns it; the developer of an app has no access to it and no way to request access.
  • It is not shared with any other person or any other app. There is no way for one user of this app to see another user's writing, because the app has no accounts, no sharing, no publishing and no feed.

If you are not signed into iCloud, or you turn iCloud off for this app in your iPhone's settings, the app works exactly the same on your device alone.

The microphone

Journal with Will does not use the microphone. This version does not ask for microphone or speech permission, and cannot: the app declares neither, so iOS would refuse it.

If a future version adds dictation, it will ask you first, this policy will say exactly what it does before that version ships, and the audio will be transcribed on your own phone and discarded rather than stored or sent anywhere.

Notifications

If you choose a daily writing reminder, it is scheduled locally on your device by iOS. Nothing about it leaves your phone. You can change the time or turn it off at any moment in the app's Settings.

The app only asks for notification permission after you have chosen a reminder time — never at first launch, and never on a new device where a reminder time arrived from iCloud.

What the app never records

Absence. The app is built so that time away is not a failure, and it follows that nothing anywhere — on the device, in iCloud, or on any screen — keeps a count of the days you did not write. There is no such number to collect, because none is ever written down. The same is true of the day's writing prompt: nothing records whether you used it, ignored it, or asked for another.

Children

Journal with Will is rated 4+. It shows no ads, has no chat, no links out, no purchases, and collects no data from anyone — including children.

Changes

If a future version of the app ever changes any of the above, this policy will be updated first, and the change will be described in plain language in the App Store release notes.
