Journal with Will

Write a line. Keep a friend.

Journal with Will is an iPhone app for keeping a journal a little at a time. Write an entry and you earn a souvenir for the volume you are filling, and some ink to spend on ordinary clothes and furniture for a small study, where a bear called Will is waiting. Nothing is ever taken away — leave it a day, a week, a month, and your place is still kept.

Support

If something isn't working, or you have a question or an idea, write to minchangyi@me.com. It's one person reading the mail, so a reply may take a few days, but every message is read.

Common questions

  • Does it cost anything? No. There is nothing to buy inside it either.
  • Do I need an account? No. There's no sign-up, no login, and no password to forget.
  • Can I write by speaking? Yes. There's a small microphone on the journal page. The transcription happens on your iPhone, and the audio is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • I got a new phone — do I lose everything? No. If you use iCloud, your journal comes with you automatically.
  • I stopped writing for a while. Nothing was lost. Your entries, souvenirs, ink and records are exactly where you left them. The app keeps no count of the days you didn't write.
  • Can I read back what I wrote? Yes — tap the bookshelf in the study, or the "Volumes filled" count on the Progress screen.

Privacy

The app collects nothing at all, and what you write goes nowhere except your own iCloud. The full policy is here: Privacy Policy.
